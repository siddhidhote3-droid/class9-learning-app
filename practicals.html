🔬 SCIENCE PRACTICALS

1. Laws of Motion

Aim: To study Newton’s laws of motion.

Procedure: Observe motion of objects under applied force.

Result: Laws of motion are verified.

2. Work and Energy

Aim: To understand work and energy.

Procedure: Lift objects and observe energy change.

Result: Energy is conserved.

3. Current Flow

Aim: To observe flow of electric current.

Procedure: Connect cell, wire and bulb.

Result: Bulb glows showing current flow.

4. Measurement of Matter

Aim: To measure mass and volume.

Procedure: Use balance and measuring cylinder.

Result: Matter has mass and volume.

5. Acids, Bases and Salts

Aim: To identify acids and bases.

Procedure: Use litmus paper.

Result: Acids turn blue litmus red.

6. Classification of Plants

Aim: To study plant classification.

Procedure: Observe plant specimens.

Result: Plants classified correctly.

7. Energy Flow in Ecosystem

Aim: To study energy flow.

Procedure: Draw food chain.

Result: Energy flows unidirectionally.

8. Useful and Harmful Microbes

Aim: To identify microbes.

Procedure: Study charts/models.

Result: Useful and harmful microbes identified.

9. Environmental Science

Aim: To study environmental components.

Procedure: Observe surroundings.

Result: Environmental balance understood.

10. Reflection of Light

Aim: To verify laws of reflection.

Procedure: Use plane mirror.

Result: Angle of incidence equals angle of reflection.

11. Study of Sound

Aim: To study sound vibrations.

Procedure: Use tuning fork.

Result: Sound produced by vibrations.

12. Life Processes

Aim: To study life processes.

Procedure: Observe charts.

Result: Life processes understood.

13. Heredity and Variations

Aim: To study heredity.

Procedure: Observe traits.

Result: Variations observed.

14. Biotechnology

Aim: To study biotechnology applications.

Procedure: Study examples.

Result: Biotechnology importance understood.

15. Observing Space Telescope

Aim: To study space observation.

Procedure: Observe images.

Result: Space objects studied.
