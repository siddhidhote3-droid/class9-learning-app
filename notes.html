🔬 Science Lessons

Laws of Motion

  • Force causes change in motion.
  • First Law: Body remains in rest or motion unless external force acts.
  • Second Law: F = ma
  • Third Law: Every action has equal and opposite reaction.
  • Momentum p = mv

Work and Energy

  • Work = Force × Displacement
  • Kinetic Energy = ½mv²
  • Potential Energy = mgh
  • Law of conservation of energy

Current Flow

  • Electric current is flow of charge
  • I = Q / t
  • SI unit: Ampere

Measurement of Matter

  • Matter has mass and volume
  • Density = Mass / Volume

Acids, Bases and Salts

  • Acids turn blue litmus red
  • Bases turn red litmus blue
  • pH scale measures acidity

Classification of Plants

  • Based on structure and reproduction
  • Thallophyta, Bryophyta, Pteridophyta

Energy Flow in Ecosystem

  • Energy flows Sun → Plants → Animals
  • 10% energy law

Useful and Harmful Microbes

  • Useful: curd, medicine
  • Harmful: diseases

Environmental Science

  • Air, water, soil form environment
  • Pollution harms life

Reflection of Light

  • Angle of incidence = angle of reflection

Study of Sound

  • Sound is produced by vibrations
  • Frequency → pitch

Life Processes

  • Nutrition, respiration, excretion

Heredity and Variations

  • Traits pass from parents
  • Variations cause evolution

Introduction to Biotechnology

  • Use of organisms for useful products

Observing Space – Telescope

  • Used to study distant objects
