State Board • Science & Mathematics

🔬 Science

  • Force causes change in motion.
  • First Law: Body remains at rest or uniform motion.
  • Second Law: F = ma
  • Third Law: Action = Reaction
  • Momentum = mv
  • Work = Force × Displacement
  • Kinetic Energy = ½mv²
  • Potential Energy = mgh
  • Law of conservation of energy
  • Electric current is flow of charge.
  • I = Q / t
  • Unit: Ampere
  • Matter has mass and volume.
  • Density = Mass / Volume
  • Acids turn blue litmus red.
  • Bases turn red litmus blue.
  • pH scale measures acidity.
  • Plants classified based on structure.
  • Groups: Thallophyta, Bryophyta, Pteridophyta.
  • Energy flows Sun → Plants → Animals.
  • 10% energy transfer rule.
  • Useful microbes help in food & medicine.
  • Harmful microbes cause diseases.
  • Environment includes air, water & soil.
  • Pollution harms environment.
  • Light travels in straight line.
  • Angle of incidence = angle of reflection.
  • Sound produced by vibrations.
  • Frequency → pitch, Amplitude → loudness.
  • Nutrition, respiration, excretion.
  • Essential for survival.
  • Traits passed from parents to offspring.
  • Variations cause evolution.
  • Use of organisms for useful products.
  • Used in medicine & agriculture.
  • Used to observe distant celestial objects.
  • Collects and focuses light.

  • Ratio compares quantities.
  • Proportion shows equality.

  • Income, expenditure & savings.
