Science & Mathematics – Detailed Notes

🔬 SCIENCE

  • Force causes change in motion.
  • First Law: Body remains at rest or uniform motion.
  • Second Law: F = ma
  • Third Law: Action = Reaction
  • Momentum p = mv
  • Work = Force × Displacement
  • Kinetic Energy = ½mv²
  • Potential Energy = mgh
  • Law of conservation of energy
  • Electric current = flow of charge
  • I = Q / t
  • Unit: Ampere
  • Closed circuit required
  • Matter has mass and volume
  • Mass measured in kg
  • Density = Mass / Volume
  • Acids turn blue litmus red
  • Bases turn red litmus blue
  • Salts from acid + base
  • pH scale measures acidity
  • Based on structure
  • Thallophyta, Bryophyta, Pteridophyta
  • Makes study easy
  • Sun → Plants → Animals
  • Food chain shows energy transfer
  • 10% energy rule
  • Useful microbes help digestion
  • Used in medicines
  • Harmful microbes cause diseases
  • Includes air, water, soil
  • Pollution harms environment
  • Conservation is important
  • Light travels in straight line
  • Angle of incidence = reflection
  • Mirrors reflect light
  • Sound produced by vibrations
  • Frequency → pitch
  • Amplitude → loudness
  • Nutrition, respiration, excretion
  • Essential for survival
  • Traits pass from parents
  • Variations cause evolution
  • Use of organisms for products
  • Used in agriculture & medicine
  • Observe distant objects
  • Collect light from space

  • Collection of well-defined objects
  • Represented by { }
  • Rational & irrational numbers
  • Euclid’s division lemma
  • Variables & coefficients
  • Degree = highest power
  • Ratio compares quantities
  • Proportion shows equality
  • ax + by + c = 0
  • Graph is straight line
  • Point has no dimension
  • Line has length only
  • Sum of angles = 180°
  • SSS, SAS, ASA rules
  • All points equidistant
  • Diameter = 2 × radius
  • sinθ = P/H
  • cosθ = B/H
  • tanθ = P/B
  • Cube = a³
  • Cuboid = l×b×h
  • Cylinder = πr²h
