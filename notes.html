🔬 SCIENCE – DETAILED NOTES

Laws of Motion

  • Motion is caused by force.
  • Newton’s First Law: Body remains at rest or uniform motion unless external force acts.
  • Second Law: Force = mass × acceleration (F = ma)
  • Third Law: Every action has equal and opposite reaction.
  • Momentum = mass × velocity (p = mv)

Work and Energy

  • Work is done when force produces displacement.
  • Work = Force × Displacement (W = F × s)
  • Kinetic Energy = ½mv²
  • Potential Energy = mgh
  • Law of conservation of energy: Energy cannot be created or destroyed.

Current Flow

  • Electric current is flow of electric charge.
  • Current (I) = Charge / Time
  • Unit of current is Ampere.
  • Closed circuit allows current to flow.

Measurement of Matter

  • Matter has mass and volume.
  • Mass is measured in kg.
  • Volume of liquids measured using measuring cylinder.
  • Density = Mass / Volume

Acids, Bases and Salts

  • Acids turn blue litmus red.
  • Bases turn red litmus blue.
  • Salts are formed from acid + base reaction.
  • pH scale measures acidity.

Classification of Plants

  • Plants classified based on structure.
  • Major groups: Thallophyta, Bryophyta, Pteridophyta.
  • Classification helps in easy study.

Energy Flow in Ecosystem

  • Energy flows from sun → plants → animals.
  • Food chain shows energy transfer.
  • Only 10% energy transfers to next level.

Useful and Harmful Microbes

  • Useful microbes help in digestion and medicine.
  • Harmful microbes cause diseases.
  • Vaccination prevents diseases.

Environmental Science

  • Environment includes air, water, soil and organisms.
  • Pollution affects environment.
  • Conservation is important.

Reflection of Light

  • Light travels in straight line.
  • Angle of incidence = Angle of reflection.
  • Mirror reflects light.

Study of Sound

  • Sound is produced by vibrations.
  • Frequency determines pitch.
  • Amplitude determines loudness.

Life Processes

  • Nutrition, respiration, excretion are life processes.
  • They help survival of organism.

Heredity and Variations

  • Traits pass from parents to offspring.
  • Variations cause evolution.

Biotechnology

  • Use of organisms for useful products.
  • Used in medicine and agriculture.

Space Telescope

  • Telescopes help observe space.
  • They collect light from distant objects.

📐 MATHEMATICS – DETAILED NOTES & FORMULAS

Sets

  • Collection of well-defined objects.
  • Represented by { }.
  • Types: finite, infinite, empty set.

Real Numbers

  • Includes rational and irrational numbers.
  • Euclid’s division lemma: a = bq + r

Polynomials

  • Polynomial has variables and coefficients.
  • Degree = highest power of variable.

Ratio and Proportion

  • Ratio compares two quantities.
  • Proportion shows equality of ratios.

Linear Equations in Two Variables

  • Form: ax + by + c = 0
  • Graph is straight line.

Financial Planning

  • Involves income and expenditure.
  • Savings help future security.

Statics

  • Study of forces on bodies at rest.
  • Balanced forces keep object stationary.

Basic Concepts in Geometry

  • Point has no dimension.
  • Line has length only.

Parallel Lines

  • Never meet.
  • Angles formed are equal.

Triangles

  • Sum of angles = 180°
  • Congruency rules: SSS, SAS, ASA.

Construction of Triangle

  • Constructed using compass and ruler.
  • Accuracy is important.

Quadrilateral

  • Four sided figure.
  • Parallelogram opposite sides equal.

Circle

  • All points equidistant from centre.
  • Diameter = 2 × radius.

Coordinate Geometry

  • Point represented as (x, y).
  • Plane divided into 4 quadrants.

Trigonometry

  • sin θ = Perpendicular / Hypotenuse
  • cos θ = Base / Hypotenuse
  • tan θ = Perpendicular / Base

Surface Area and Volume

  • Cube volume = a³
  • Cuboid volume = l × b × h
  • Cylinder volume = πr²h
