Class 9 Academic Projects

Science & Mathematics – State Board

🔬 SCIENCE PROJECTS

1. Laws of Motion in Daily Life

Objective: To study applications of Newton’s Laws of Motion.

Methodology: Observation of real-life examples like walking, cycling and vehicles.

Conclusion: Newton’s laws are applicable in everyday activities.

2. Conservation of Energy

Objective: To understand conservation of energy.

Materials: Ball, inclined plane.

Conclusion: Energy can neither be created nor destroyed.

3. Electrical Energy and Its Uses

Objective: To study electric current and household appliances.

Conclusion: Electrical energy plays an important role in modern life.

4. Acids, Bases and Salts in Everyday Life

Objective: To identify acidic and basic substances.

Conclusion: Chemical substances are widely used in daily life.

5. Classification of Plants

Objective: To study plant classification based on characteristics.

Conclusion: Classification helps in systematic study of plants.

6. Energy Flow in an Ecosystem

Objective: To understand food chain and food web.

Conclusion: Energy flows in one direction in ecosystem.

7. Role of Microorganisms

Objective: To study useful and harmful microorganisms.

Conclusion: Microorganisms are beneficial as well as harmful.

8. Environmental Pollution

Objective: To study types of pollution and prevention.

Conclusion: Pollution control is essential for sustainable development.

9. Reflection of Light

Objective: To study laws of reflection.

Conclusion: Laws of reflection are verified.

10. Sound and Its Applications

Objective: To study production and transmission of sound.

Conclusion: Sound travels through vibrations.

11. Life Processes in Living Organisms

Objective: To understand nutrition, respiration and excretion.

Conclusion: Life processes are essential for survival.

12. Heredity and Variation

Objective: To study inheritance of traits.

Conclusion: Variations help in evolution.

13. Introduction to Biotechnology

Objective: To study applications of biotechnology.

Conclusion: Biotechnology has wide applications in medicine and agriculture.

14. Space Exploration and Telescopes

Objective: To study space observation tools.

Conclusion: Telescopes help in studying celestial objects.
